# Thank you

VORTHYX exists because people kept asking questions, trying things, and sharing what they found.

Thank you to everyone who followed my story and offered kindness; to the people who suggested features, found bugs, and patiently tested fixes; and to everyone who made a fork and gave an idea a second life. Personal support and technical contributions both matter to me.

## Project lineage

- [HEXPYRION](https://github.com/DimaReverse/hexpyrion): the earlier static recovery project that VORTHYX continues.
- [REVENANT](https://github.com/DimaReverse/nuitka-revenant): earlier work on native-to-Python reconstruction and recovery audits.
- [Nuitka/Themida unpacker](https://github.com/DimaReverse/nuitka-themida-unpacker): related work on a separate unpacking workflow; not bundled here.

These projects are by DimaReverse. They remain part of the history of this work.

## Tooling

Thanks to the maintainers of Python, Capstone, pefile, Zstandard, xdis, Unicorn, pytest, and the wider reverse-engineering ecosystem. Optional tools have their own licenses and support boundaries. Mentioning a project does not imply its endorsement.

## Community credits and forks

This page deliberately avoids guessing identities or attributing unverified fixes. To add a contribution or fork, submit a pull request with a public issue, commit, pull request, or repository link and a short description. Use only the public name you want displayed; please do not publish anyone else's private details.
